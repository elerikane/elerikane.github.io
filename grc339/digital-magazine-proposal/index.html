Introduction

Silver Screen

My magazine will be from the company Silver Screen. The goal of the site is to highlight and explore stories that people are currently watching and talking about, from intense TV series like The Pitt and The Bear to visually striking and thought-provoking films like Project Hail Mary, Whiplash, and Inception. I’m focusing on creating a clean, modern layout that reflects the cinematic feel of the content it features. The website will include sections for reviews, featured articles, and updates on trending releases, giving users an easy way to discover and learn more about different shows and films.
