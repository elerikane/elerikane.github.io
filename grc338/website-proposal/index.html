Company Introduction

Kane Gallery & Studio

Kane Gallery & Studio is a contemporary art space that blends curated fine artwork with personalized commission services. Located in the heart of San Jose, California, the gallery showcases original paintings, limited-edition prints, and bespoke commissioned pieces created by resident artist Kane. The gallery is both an exhibition space and a working studio, allowing visitors to experience the artistic process while also commissioning meaningful, one-of-a-kind artwork tailored specifically to them.

About

About the Artist Kane is a contemporary portrait artist whose work focuses on environment and figures. Her pieces blend realism with expressive detail, creating timeless works that feel both intimate and refined. After years of private commissions, Kane opened her gallery to create a space where visitors could experience art in person while also collaborating directly with the artist. Each piece displayed in the gallery is original and handcrafted. Commissioned works are designed in collaboration with clients to reflect their memories, relationships, and imagination.
